## 2026-10-22 09:00 IST · ig07 · ALMM explained - a list, not a subsidy

Format: carousel · Pillar: Policy Explained · Assets: media/out/posts/ig07/

```
ALMM is one of the most important solar policies most investors have never read.

It doesn't subsidise anything. It's a list. If you're not on it, government-linked projects can't buy from you.

List-I covered modules. List-II (cells) took effect June 2026. That shifts the scarce, higher-margin part of the chain upstream.
```
