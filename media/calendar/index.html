Strategy, cadence and guardrails

Positioning

  • "See the supply chain." Map the layer, then the company.
  • Audience: serious retail investors, finance students, analysts and operators.
  • Voice: plain, data-first, never hype. Every number carries a source.

Content pillars

  • Supply Chain Decoded: one sector, layer by layer
  • Numbers that Matter: charts, stats, rule-based screens
  • Policy Explained · Myth vs Data · Bottleneck Watch
  • Hidden Connections: one company, many chains
  • Product and build-in-public

Cadence (IST)

  • Instagram: 09:00 carousel (saves) + 19:30 Reel or quick-hit
  • LinkedIn (founder profile): 08:45 daily, link in first comment
  • Substack "The Map": alternate Sundays, 10:00
  • Week 1: AI, EVs, solar, power. Week 2: ships, jets, trains, chips, 5G

Compliance guardrails

  • Education only. No buy/sell/hold, targets or return claims (SEBI finfluencer rules).
  • Screens always state their rule and "not recommendations".
  • Seed figures are as of 25 Sep 2026. Re-check against the live card before posting.
  • Disclaimer on every post and email.

What to measure

  • Instagram: saves and shares per reach; profile → link taps
  • LinkedIn: comments, reposts, profile visits
  • Substack: open rate, new subscribers per issue
  • Site: UTM sessions and sign-ups by post (utm_content = post id)
