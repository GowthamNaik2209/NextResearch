## Post (feed, ~170 words)

How can you build a product alone with the help of AI tools? It's very doable today - but the tools are the easy part. The process around them is what makes it work.

I wrote up what I've learned from building NextResearch this way, as a guide for anyone starting out:

→ Prototype in a chat window first. Move to a real codebase once you know what you want.
→ Expect one painful setup day to get the site online. After that, releasing is a sentence.
→ Keep a private test copy of your site, separate from the live one.
→ Let users decide what gets built. Keep an idea list and let ideas wait.
→ Half-baked ideas are fine. Ask "what do you think?" and let the suggestions sharpen them.
→ Ask for a mock-up before any big change.
→ Test every change across phones, tablets and laptops - first with a second AI, then yourself.

The full write-up covers prompting styles, testing and a starter checklist. There's also an interactive guide you can click through.

Would love to hear what's working for others building this way. You can see the result at nextresearchapp.in.
