Where ideas come from

Keep an idea list. Let users point the way.

1

Hear it

A user or friend asks for something, often in different words.

2

Write it down

An idea list in any notes app. Let it sit until it's had real thinking time.

3

Bring references

Saved social posts and app screenshots show the look you want.

4

Mock it up

The AI makes a mock-up in your brand's colours. You react.

5

Build and test

A second AI tests it on phones, tablets and laptops. Then you do.

6

Show the same people

Adjust, then make it live.

Same question, three ways

When several users ask for the same thing in different words, that's your next feature. On NextResearch, backtesting came from users asking "would buying these have worked?"

The next question

A shipped feature creates the next request. Once users could see past returns, they asked for a forward range, which became projections.

The missing view

Listen for "it shows me X but not Y". A news page that missed institutional money flows became a flows dashboard.
